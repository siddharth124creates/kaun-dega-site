Privacy policy

Last updated 8 October 2026. Short version: we keep what's needed to split bills with your friends, nothing more, and we never sell it.

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics: name, email address and profile photo link, when you sign in with Google. We never see your Google password.
  • What you put in the app: group names, the names of friends you add, kharche (amounts, titles, who paid, how they were split), payments you record, reactions, comments and reminders.
  • Your UPI id, only if you add it, so friends can pay you. Friends' UPI ids you type in are stored the same way.
  • Your phone number, only if you add it in Settings. It is shown on your own Profile and is not shown to friends.

We don't ask for contacts, SMS, photos, location or bank details. Payments happen in your own UPI app; we never touch or hold money.

Who can see it

People in a group see that group's members, kharche, payments, comments and the UPI ids of its members. Nobody outside the group can see it. A group's invite link lets someone join it, so share it only with the people you split with.

Where it's stored

On Supabase's servers in Mumbai, India. Sign-in uses Google. The app is downloaded from GitHub. Each of these runs its own logs under its own policy.

What we don't do

  • No ads, and we don't sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs in the app today. If we add crash reports later, this page will say so first.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a kharcha or a group you made from inside the app; it is gone for everyone in it.
  • To delete your account, open Settings in the app and tap Delete account. Your profile, phone and UPI id are removed straight away. Groups you made pass to the next friend on the app, or are deleted if nobody else is on it. Kharche you shared in friends' groups stay under your name, so their totals still add up.
  • If you can't open the app, write to the contact on the home page from the email you sign in with and we'll do it within 30 days.

Children

Kaun Dega? is meant for people 13 and older.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes on this page with a new date before it applies.
