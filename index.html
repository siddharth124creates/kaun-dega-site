Sach mein free hai?

Haan bhai, sach mein. Kharcha splitting, groups, reminders aur UPI settle-up, sab free aur bina limit ke. Baad mein receipt scan jaise kuch extra features aa sakte hain, par jo aaj free hai wo free hi rahega.

Play Store pe kyun nahi hai?

Aayega, pakka. Beta mein hum app seedha share kar rahe hain taaki bugs jaldi fix ho sakein. Public launch pe yeh button Play Store pe le jaayega.

Kaun Dega mera paisa rakhta hai?

Nahi. Settle up karte hi tumhara apna UPI app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, koi bhi) khulta hai aur paisa seedha dost ko jaata hai. Hum sirf note karte hain ki kharcha chukta ho gaya.

Update kaise milega?

App khulte hi check karta hai. Naya version aaya toh "Naya version aaya hai" ke saath Download button dikhega. Tumhare groups aur kharche account mein safe rehte hain.

iPhone app kab aayega?

Abhi nahi. Pehle Android, public launch ke baad iPhone. Tab tak dost ke Android pe kaam chalao.
